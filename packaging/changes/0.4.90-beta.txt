- Propagates shutdown cancellation through provider work and drains tracked Arena operations before exit.
- Constrains the Live Agents viewport, releases empty toolbar space, preserves keyboard focus on rail collapse, and bounds the 3D material cache.
- Restricts release packaging to win-x64 while the pinned CPython and wheel payload remains architecture-specific.
- Prevents payload smoke builds from mutating finalized release directories and invalidating checksum manifests.
