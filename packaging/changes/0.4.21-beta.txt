AI Arena 0.4.21-beta

- Added AI World lock metadata to 3D avatars and surfaced locked agents with visible badge geometry.
- Added AI World voice-style metadata to 3D avatars and rendered non-default voice cues on styled agents.
- Added AI World pressure-profile metadata to 3D avatars and rendered pressure bands on constrained agents.
- Added a distinct narrator booth identity treatment so narrator turns read as ringside commentary instead of another generic robot.
- Added richer tool activity props in the 3D arena for agents using internet/tool workflows.
- Added internet-source activity props in the 3D arena for agents with recent sourced web activity.
- Added error beacon props in the 3D arena for agents with warning or failure states.
- Added compact speaker and turn headers inside AI World speech bubbles for faster live-dialogue scanning.
- Expanded AI World inspector detail with voice style, pressure profile, and locked state.
- Expanded AI World event chips with narrator, locked, voice, and pressure cues.
- Added WPF regression coverage for avatar voice/pressure/lock mapping, narrator identity geometry, activity props, bubble headers, and inspector chips.
