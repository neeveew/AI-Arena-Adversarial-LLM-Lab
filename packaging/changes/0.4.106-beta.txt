AI Arena 0.4.106-beta

- Adds `Get-AIArenaCollaborateReview` and `collaborate.review` for saved final answers, deterministic review verdicts, aggregate metrics, and complete latest-turn traces.
- Moves the Collaborate control-plane command family into a dedicated auditable handler.
- Fixes false `Needs review` verdicts caused only by healthy restored, exported, or saved-run lifecycle labels.
- Keeps a fresh authoritative application `State` snapshot attached to every authenticated PowerShell response, including review failures.
